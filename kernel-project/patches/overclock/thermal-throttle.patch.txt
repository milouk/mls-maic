    maic: cpufreq: thermal throttle must lower the frequency, not just the core count
    
    Two vendor behaviours in mtk_power_throttle.c left the SoC without a working
    thermostat once MTK hotplug is off (perf.sh) or a frequency floor is set.
    
    1. mt_cpufreq_thermal_protect() searches the power table without matching the
       core count (the match is commented out in the MT8167 tree), so in a table
       sorted by power the first entry under budget is "few cores at a high
       frequency". Measured 2026-09-17 (ceiling soak: 4 threads, interactive,
       max 1400000, min 598000, HPS off): ATM cut the CPU budget from 1281 to
       300 mW within seconds of crossing 80.4 C, and 300 mW resolved to 1 core @
       1400 MHz. hps_set_cpu_num_limit() is a no-op with HPS disabled, so
       clipped_freq stayed 1400000, the frequency never moved and the SoC reached
       90.7 C. Match the core count again (prefer keeping the cores and lowering
       the frequency) and skip the unused zero OPP slots of opp_tbl_default.
    
    2. The cpufreq policy notifier refuses any clip below policy->min. With a
       floor of 1300000 (the intended configuration: interactive governor, 1300
       floor, 1500 ceiling) -- or merely the transient 1300000/1196000 floors
       PerfService writes during boosts -- thermal could never go below 1300.
       Let the clip override the floor; the floor comes back by itself when the
       limit is released, because cpufreq_update_policy() rebuilds the policy
       from user_policy. clipped_freq == 0 (no limit computed yet) never clips.
    
    Both under CONFIG_MAIC_CPU_OC; the vendor code is unchanged otherwise.
